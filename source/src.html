every creator video, decoded.

Point Anatomi at any brand and its AI breaks down every creator video on TikTok and Instagram, then shows what worked and why.

I built it from scratch, pairing software development with what agency and newsroom work taught me about marketing, branding and advertising. Brands have used it internationally to brief their next round of creators on evidence instead of gut feel.

Anatomi was acquired by Aether AI, a venture and AI studio, and now runs as an Aether AI product.

what it does

A UGC campaign decoder. It collects every creator video for a brand, transcribes it, sorts it into the formats creators repeat and turns the results into a branded report with a creative brief for the next round.
